# GTM Future Sprints

The three themes are fixed: validate usefulness with governance practitioners, pilot handoff into an AI inventory, and test paid demand.

## Sprint 1 ? Validate usefulness with governance practitioners
- Goal: confirm that the report format and checklist coverage help governance reviewers make faster triage decisions.
- Audience: governance practitioners, AI PMO leads, and risk/compliance reviewers.
- Channel: 30-minute interviews and a lightweight follow-up survey.
- Deliverable: revised demo script, prioritized feedback log, and two annotated sample reports.
- Success criteria: at least 5 practitioner interviews completed; at least 3 reviewers say the report is useful for first-pass triage; at least 2 specific wording improvements are identified.
- Target metrics: interview completion rate 100% for scheduled sessions; usefulness rating target of 4/5 or higher.

## Sprint 2 ? Pilot handoff into an AI inventory
- Goal: test whether the brief can feed an AI inventory or intake register without manual reformatting.
- Audience: AI inventory owners, governance ops, and platform admins.
- Channel: a guided handoff session with one pilot team.
- Deliverable: an inventory-ready export map, field mapping notes, and a pilot handoff checklist.
- Success criteria: one pilot handoff completed; at least 80% of required fields mapped without custom rework; the pilot team can file one model entry using the exported data.
- Target metrics: handoff time target under 15 minutes; field mapping completeness target of 90%.

## Sprint 3 ? Test paid demand
- Goal: determine whether teams will pay for a supported version of the brief and adjacent services.
- Audience: compliance leaders, procurement stakeholders, and team sponsors with budget authority.
- Channel: pricing conversations, a short pilot offer, and a follow-up willingness-to-pay survey.
- Deliverable: pricing hypothesis, pilot offer language, and a list of qualified prospects.
- Success criteria: at least 3 pricing conversations completed; at least 2 prospects express purchase intent; at least 1 prospect agrees to a next-step proposal review.
- Target metrics: conversion target of 20% from qualified prospect to proposal review; willingness-to-pay signal target from at least 2 prospects.
