# GTM Future Sprints

Three sprints after the MVP, in order: validate usefulness with governance practitioners, pilot the handoff into an AI inventory, and test paid demand. Each sprint is about reaching users and learning whether they value the brief, not about building features.

## Sprint 1: Validate usefulness with governance practitioners
- Goal: confirm that the report format and checklist coverage help governance reviewers make faster triage decisions.
- Target user / buyer: governance practitioners, AI PMO leads, and risk/compliance reviewers.
- Channel or motion: 30-minute interviews and a lightweight follow-up survey.
- Key deliverable: revised demo script, prioritized feedback log, and two annotated sample reports.
- Success metric: at least 5 practitioner interviews completed; at least 3 reviewers say the report is useful for first-pass triage; at least 2 specific wording improvements are identified.
- Additional targets: interview completion rate 100% for scheduled sessions; usefulness rating target of 4/5 or higher.

## Sprint 2: Pilot handoff into an AI inventory
- Goal: test whether the brief can feed an AI inventory or intake register without manual reformatting.
- Target user / buyer: AI inventory owners, governance ops, and platform admins.
- Channel or motion: a guided handoff session with one pilot team.
- Key deliverable: an inventory-ready export map, field mapping notes, and a pilot handoff checklist.
- Success metric: one pilot handoff completed; at least 80% of required fields mapped without custom rework; the pilot team can file one model entry using the exported data.
- Additional targets: handoff time target under 15 minutes; field mapping completeness target of 90%.

## Sprint 3: Test paid demand
- Goal: determine whether teams will pay for a supported version of the brief and adjacent services.
- Target user / buyer: compliance leaders, procurement stakeholders, and team sponsors with budget authority.
- Channel or motion: pricing conversations, a short pilot offer, and a follow-up willingness-to-pay survey.
- Key deliverable: pricing hypothesis, pilot offer language, and a list of qualified prospects.
- Success metric: at least 3 pricing conversations completed; at least 2 prospects express purchase intent; at least 1 prospect agrees to a next-step proposal review.
- Additional targets: conversion target of 20% from qualified prospect to proposal review; willingness-to-pay signal target from at least 2 prospects.
